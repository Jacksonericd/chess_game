# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 11, 2026

Chess App (the “App”) is a single-player chess game published by Chess App / com.jed (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the App.

## Information the App handles

### Information you provide

The App does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

### Game information

Games, moves, difficulty settings, and board preferences are used to provide the game experience. The current version keeps this information locally in the running App and does not upload it to our servers.

### Analytics and diagnostics

The App uses Firebase services. Depending on the Firebase project configuration and your device settings, Firebase Analytics may automatically receive app-usage and diagnostic information such as app launches, approximate location, device and operating-system information, language, app version, and interactions with the App. We use this information to understand usage, monitor performance, and improve the App. We do not use it to identify you by name.

### Advertising

The App uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner and app-open advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as an advertising ID or other device identifiers, IP address, device and app information, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements. Depending on your location, settings, and available consent controls, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google’s services operate under Google’s own privacy policies and terms. See [Google’s Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google’s advertising settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

## How information is used

Information handled through the App and its service providers may be used to:

- provide and maintain the chess game;
- display and measure advertisements;
- understand aggregate usage and improve features;
- detect fraud, abuse, crashes, and technical problems; and
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

## Sharing

We do not sell information that you provide directly to us. We share information with service providers only as needed to operate the App, including Google Firebase and Google Mobile Ads. These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or our legal rights.

You can limit analytics or advertising activity by disabling permissions or changing privacy settings offered by your device or platform. Some App functionality and advertisements may still work differently when these choices are used.

If the App presents a consent or privacy choice in your region, you can use that choice to grant, refuse, or withdraw consent as described there.
